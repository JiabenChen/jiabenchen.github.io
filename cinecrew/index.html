Method

Multi-agent cinematic production from script to film

Our framework organizes long-form generation as a virtual production studio, where specialized agents collaborate over story, acting, camera language, asset control, audio, and post-production decisions.

Abstract

Better Call CineCrew tackles ultra-long narrative-to-film generation with a multi-agent production framework that decomposes the filmmaking process into specialized roles. Instead of treating long video generation as a single monolithic synthesis problem, our system coordinates story analysis, cinematic planning, character and scene memory, acting direction, voice control, execution, and post-production into a coherent pipeline. This design improves long-range consistency in identity, shot structure, narrative flow, and audiovisual presentation across multi-shot sequences.
